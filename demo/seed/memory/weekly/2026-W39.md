# 2026-W39

## Summary
During week 2026‑W39 the bakery was closed for a Monday holiday, evaluated flour suppliers after receiving quotes and a test batch, and saw chestnut croissants sell out daily. The owner preferred Mill Co’s flour, requested a loyalty discount, and postponed a third‑oven purchase pending October sales.

- Owner considered a third oven (Sato quote ¥1.2M, ¥350k deposit) but decided to wait for October numbers due to spend.limit cap

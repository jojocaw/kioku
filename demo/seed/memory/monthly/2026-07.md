## Lessons
- Lemon cream buns at ¥320 sell out quickly, indicating strong price‑point acceptance.
- Early‑morning lamination prevents butter softening in high heat, improving product quality.
- Regular butter stock checks are essential to avoid shortages given twice‑weekly deliveries.
- Wifi password must be stored securely to prevent card‑reader disruptions during peak sales.
- Sunday work affects family time; adjusting schedule can alleviate fatigue.
- Listening to staff requests for schedule changes improves coverage and morale.
- Monitoring sales trends helps align production with actual demand (e.g., croissant target 280‑300).

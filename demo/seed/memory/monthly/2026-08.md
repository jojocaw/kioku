# 2026-08

## Summary
In August 2026, July sales totaled ¥1.86M while August sales fell to ¥1.72M due to the Obon closure and a week with one oven. The shop set a record croissant sale of 295 units on August 1 and saw strong iced‑coffee demand during the hot spell. Key actions included approving a new weekday schedule for Aiko starting September 1, hiring weekend helper Yui at ¥1,200/hour effective September 5, and stepping back from Sunday counter duties.

## Lessons
- Hot weather significantly boosts both croissant and iced‑coffee sales
- Oven malfunctions directly cut production and cause staff discomfort
- Social‑media content (e.g., Instagram lamination video) drives follower growth and walk‑in traffic far more effectively than printed flyers
- Flyer distribution with coupons yielded very low redemption, proving ineffective compared to a single viral video
- Free community‑board/window signage can successfully attract weekend‑helper applicants without paid ads
- Adjusting staff schedules (e.g., shifting butter deliveries, revising Sunday work) improves workflow and meets personal time goals
- The Obon closure markedly reduces local foot traffic and sales, requiring advance planning for reduced output
- Consistent monitoring of equipment (oven thermostat, walk‑in fridge noise) and supplier routines prevents unexpected disruptions

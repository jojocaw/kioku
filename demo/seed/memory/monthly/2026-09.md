# 2026-09

## Summary
September sales reached a record ¥2.05M, up from August's ¥1.72M, driven by the autumn menu launch and strong weekend performance. The owner held the croissant price at ¥380 despite an 8% flour cost increase, secured a 2% loyalty discount from Mill Co, and decided to delay a third‑oven purchase pending October sales. Key operational changes included a revised start time to alleviate back pain, successful launch of chestnut croissant and pumpkin bread, and continued reliance on the weekend helper for personal time.
